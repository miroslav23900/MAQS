CHRONOLOGY LOG // 2011 WINAMP PULSE TO 2026 V6 VAULT ACCESS: UNRESTRICTED

I. 2011 IRKUTSK BLACKOUTS & THE WINAMP PULSE

MAQS engineered his 2011 debut CD (MAQS / BREAKDOWN) on an aging, overheated PC amid recurring Irkutsk power outages. The visual and sonic identity of BREAKDOWN was born from watching a blood-red heartbeat pulse inside a classic WinAmp visualization window. Track 03, BREAK THAT SH#T!, pairs a classical violin intro with a Colosseum arena concept. (SoundDeegres Records did not exist during the 2011 era.)

II. 2016 BREAKTHROUGH (910) & KEY COLLABORATORS

The 2016 album 910 marked MAQS's career breakthrough: the ringing piano bridge of Down To The Past, the raw vocal session of Clara (who arrived at the studio in tears for Don’t Trust), the children's choir, piano, acid synths, and tiger growl of FIRE!, and the inaugural collaboration with JackTrow on War In Night Tokyo. After 2018's Avatar-inspired Wonder World EP (and a 2018 lore conflict with JackTrow over Vertex) plus 2021's Through The Time (I Can’t Stand V2), the duo reunited to launch SoundDeegres Records. Other canon and vault lore references include Mersacom, Scrapers Group, Magnetude, AKOV, and SVDDEN DEATH.

SOUNDDEEGRES RECORDS // ARCHIVAL HQ

Founded in 2024 by MAQS and JackTrow as the official home for surgical bass, V6 master engineering, and archival preservation. Mounty Side is MAQS's first official label master, strictly separated from early vault prototypes Come On! (Mounty Side V1) and Italy Hype (Volume Max V1). Following the 2025 release of NO FIX, SoundDeegres permanently ended physical CD manufacturing prior to 2026's SAMANDA.
